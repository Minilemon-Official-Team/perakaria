## Scope and mode
Public single-page homepage in Experience mode with a secondary Persuade job. CMS admin is the related Operate surface.

## Audience, job, and action
Brand and production leads evaluating an Indonesian creative-technology studio. They should understand the audiovisual focus, inspect representative work and capability, then start a WhatsApp or email conversation.

## Proof and constraints
Proof is cinematic work imagery, service specificity, transparent placeholder labels, client experience structure, and direct contact routes. All visible content, imagery, brand logo/wordmark, favicon, OG image, every client logo with alt text, About Company copy, repeatable skill details and expert photos with alt text, section order/visibility/backgrounds, theme tokens, contacts, and SEO stay CMS-editable within a fixed sharp editorial system.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
User-approved visual option 3, revised in-session: a full-bleed studio signal with an oversized statement directly over a decisive cinematic portrait. Mobile reduces the jumbotron to title and description only. Global same-page navigation uses cancellable exponential smooth scrolling with responsive fixed-header offsets; it becomes instant under reduced motion or when Motion is disabled in CMS. The portfolio auto-advances on desktop, remains directly swipeable on desktop/mobile, and Capabilities uses a single-open accordion. Immediately before Contact, an editorial About Company + Expertise split reveals one expert detail plate on desktop hover/keyboard focus and opens that plate inline on mobile tap.

## Unresolved decisions
Final company facts, client names/logos, portfolio metadata, licensed PP Neue Montreal files, final domains, showreel URLs, and social links remain replacement items in CMS.
